# Zippo Butane Lighter Refill

Refilling a premium torch insert like your Zippo torch/jet lighter requires a specific technique to ensure it doesn't leak or misfire.

---

## Step 1: Safety & Environment

* **Find a good spot:** Move to a well-ventilated area (like near an open window or a kitchen counter) away from any open flames, lit cigarettes, or sparks.
* **Cool down:** Ensure the Zippo insert is completely cool to the touch before starting. Never attempt to refill a lighter immediately after using it.

## Step 2: Purge the Trapped Air (Crucial Step)

If you skip this, air pockets will get trapped inside the tank, causing the lighter to sputter or fail to ignite even when full of fuel.

* **Position the insert:** Hold the Zippo insert upside down in your non-dominant hand so the bottom brass plate is facing up toward the ceiling.
* **Locate the valve:** Look at the very bottom of the insert. You will see a small, circular brass refueling valve with a tiny pin in the center.
* **Bleed the tank:** Take a small screwdriver, a ballpoint pen, or a wooden toothpick. Press the tip of your tool straight down into that center pin.
* **Listen:** You will hear a sharp hissing sound as compressed air and residual gas escape. Maintain steady pressure until the hissing completely stops and you hear absolute silence.

## Step 3: Turn the Flame Adjuster to Minimum

* **Locate the adjuster:** On the bottom plate next to the fill valve, look for the flathead adjustment screw or plastic dial flanked by a plus **(+)** and minus **(-)** sign.
* **Lower the fuel flow:** Use a small screwdriver or a coin to turn that screw all the way toward the minus **(-)** symbol. This closes off the internal valve gasket and protects it from the sudden pressure surge of incoming liquid fuel.

## Step 4: The Refill Process

* **Prep the fuel can:** Take your butane can and give it 2 to 3 firm up-and-down shakes to prime the liquid propellant inside.
* **Align perfectly straight:** Keep the Zippo insert held upside down so the fill valve points straight up at the ceiling. Turn the butane can completely upside down as well, pointing the long white/metal nozzle straight down at the floor.
* **Connect them:** Place the tip of the butane can't nozzle directly into the center hole of the insert's refueling valve. Both the can and the lighter must be completely vertical—if you tilt either at an angle, gas will spray out sideways instead of going inside.
* **Press and hold:** Push the butane can straight down firmly into the lighter's valve. Hold it down with steady pressure for **4 to 5 seconds**. You will feel the metal body of the Zippo insert grow intensely cold in your hand—this is normal and means the liquid butane is transferring.
* **Release:** Pull the can straight up and away quickly. You might hear a momentary tiny "pfft" sound as you disconnect, which is perfectly safe.

## Step 5: The Warm-Up Rest

* **Set it down:** Do not try to ignite the lighter yet. The liquid butane inside the tank is currently freezing cold, and cold butane will not vaporize properly to reach the ignition jets.
* **Wait 3 to 5 minutes:** Let the insert sit undisturbed at room temperature. Alternatively, you can roll the insert between the palms of your hands for a minute to let your body heat warm up the fuel tank evenly.

## Step 6: Adjust and Test

* **Reset the flame level:** Take your screwdriver and turn the bottom adjustment screw back toward the plus **(+)** sign to a medium setting.
* **Ignite:** Flip the top cam up, ensure your face is a safe distance away from the burners, and depress the ignition button. You should see a crisp, powerful double torch flame. If the flame is too low or high, adjust the bottom screw slightly until it's perfectly dialed in.
